02Pricing FAQ

What people usually ask before signing up.

How do I actually sign up?

Self-serve, through the customer portal: sign in, create your organization, and Individual is active immediately — no card needed. Team is the same flow, then an Odoo-hosted checkout to add your card; we never handle card data directly. Prefer the API? The same bootstrap it's built on works too — see documentation.

What counts against the Individual cap?

Thoughts saved (capture_thought/update_thought), embedding calls (search and capture both call the embedding model), and total stored content size. All three reset monthly.

Can we move from Individual to Team later?

Yes. Your context ids, org id, and every thought already captured stay exactly as they are — moving tiers changes billing and caps, not the data.

How does billing work?

Team and Enterprise are billed per seat, per month, through our billing partner Odoo — the same billing infrastructure DFENS AI Ltd already runs for dfens.ai. We never handle card data directly.

Where is our data stored?

You choose a data-residency region at org creation: europe-west1, us-central1, or asia-northeast1. Each is a genuinely separate deployment, not a latency fan-out.

What exactly is "background scanning"?

An Enterprise entitlement: bots that automatically capture context into memory from your team's other tools, so nobody has to remember to write it down. We'll walk through exactly what it covers for your use case in the Enterprise conversation.
